# Production plan

## Workflow
1. Lock screenplay and character designs.
2. Generate character reference turnarounds and facial expressions.
3. Build audio-first animatic using voice tracks, pauses and scratch music.
4. Test 60–90 second pilot before buying additional video credits.
5. Produce action shots with image-to-video; use still-image animation, cutaways and UI graphics for dialogue.
6. Record shot status and actual cost in `docs/shotlist.csv` (generated by script).
7. Mix dialogue, original/licensed music and effects; add English subtitles.
8. Export 1080p H.264 MP4 at 24fps, AAC 48kHz and review full film.

## Runtime
25:00 including 60 seconds credits and stinger. Timings are targets; dialogue alone does not fill each scene. Add intentional reaction beats, establishing shots and music sequences; review an audio animatic before final animation.

## Quality gates
- Consistent wardrobe and faces
- Hinglish intelligibility
- No unreadable AI-generated UI: add terminal overlays in edit
- Technical events are plausible enough for comedy
- Voice/music/asset commercial-use licenses documented
- No credentials, company systems or real internal screenshots

## Weekly milestones
Week 1: references + pilot. Week 2: voices + animatic. Weeks 3–4: scenes + edit. Week 5: final mix + YouTube.
