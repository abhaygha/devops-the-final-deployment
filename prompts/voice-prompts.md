# Voice direction

All dialogue: conversational Hinglish, not robotic Hindi-English switching. Maintain one consistent voice per role. Generate each line as separate named WAV where possible.

Manager: mature resonant Indian male, pompous corporate English, increasingly shrill in emergencies.
Lead: quick mid-pitch Indian male, overly cheerful process jargon, breathless.
Senior: low dry Indian male, tired sardonic timing, sudden explosive frustration.
Junior: young upbeat Indian male, innocent sincerity, bad news delivered proudly.
Narrator: warm theatrical Indian male, cheeky movie-trailer delivery.

Mixing: preserve 300–800ms gaps around punchlines; duck music under speech; avoid clipping. Obtain commercial-use rights for voices and do not clone real people without consent.
